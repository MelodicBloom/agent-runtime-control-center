### 1. Core Frameworks & Methodologies

* **Evidence-bound completion:** Match each completion claim to evidence of the requested behavior. Keep observation, inference, implementation mapping, expected outcome, and measured result distinct. Documentation completion cannot establish runtime completion. [S1, S2]
* **Bounded inquiry and experimentation:** State the decision, scope, exclusions, acceptance criteria, comparison method, risks, and stopping condition. Prefer a reversible test when it resolves uncertainty more effectively than another question. Retain counterevidence and failures. This is a recurring methodology; numerical thresholds in draft inquiry specifications remain project-scoped. [S2, S3]
* **Regenerative design:** Evaluate future capacity, beneficiaries, burdens, dependency, reversibility, and delayed effects across repository, organization, network, and ecosystem. The inspected portfolio document proposes this framework; its approval field is NONE. Treat it as a declared design direction, not proof of adopted or achieved net-positive outcomes. [S4]
* **Systemic Constraints:** Never invent approval, verification, source access, measurements, or deployment. Preserve the scope of authorization already given. Apply specimen consent and usage boundaries to the relevant artist-reference study rather than every coding task. Record technical debt and shifted maintenance burden. Accessibility and performance limits must be specified and tested per project; no universal numerical budget or time/space-complexity rule was established by this sample. [S1–S4]

### 2. Architectural & Design Logic

* **Modularity Rules:** Give each source, contract, adapter, execution component, and projection an explicit responsibility. Trace semantic fields through integration boundaries and classify each as preserved, renamed, derived, dropped, unsupported, or unknown. Preserve existing runtime ownership when integrating new controls. Source/adapter/projection separation recurs in the inspected designs; universal UI/business-logic separation was not verified in implementation. [S4, S5]
* **Taxonomy & Naming:** Preserve exact governed vocabulary: `visual_observation`, `mechanism_hypothesis`, `implementation_mapping`, `measured_result`, `evidence_status`, `expected_outcomes`, `risks`, `exclusions`, and `experiment_receipts`. Snake case is present in studied schemas, but no 80% frequency census was conducted. Follow each repository's actual convention for variables and components. UUIDv7, uppercase relation verbs, and proposed schema URIs belong to the draft portfolio architecture and are not global requirements. [S1, S2, S4]
* **State & Data Flow:** Keep source evidence distinct from summaries, embeddings, projections, approvals, and assessments. Preserve revision and transformation lineage. Canonical tokens, composites, style labels, presets, generator adapters, measurements, relationships, constraints, and contextual annotations have distinct meanings in NVOS; experimental primitives require evidence before promotion. The ledger plus rebuildable projections architecture is a documented proposal, not a verified universal deployment. [S2, S4]
* **Stack and UI Composition:** Select tools through the existing repository and its compatibility constraints. WebGL2, Three.js, CSS/SVG, Figma, JSON/TypeScript, and Postgres appear as targets or proposed components, not a mandatory stack. Separate canonical material meaning from renderer bindings. Give human readers conversational explanations; give executors explicit files, values, contracts, and receipts. For setup, provide navigation, pasteable values, and explicit blank/unsupported fields. [S1, S5, S6]

### 3. Motion Grammar & Kinetics

* **Easing Rules:** No universal Bezier curve, spring constant, mass, friction value, duration, or stagger was established. Specify model, units, coordinate frame, parameters, and evidence status per implementation. Do not represent synthetic Bessel/polar research as an artist's production method. [S2]
* **Choreography:** Keep authored motion intent and provider bindings traceable; verify endpoint and loop claims against the machine representation. Define reduced-motion and fallback behavior for each visual deliverable. Preserve any explicitly contracted input-modality limit. A single-input artist study does not prohibit autonomous time evolution. Historical determinism claims require their own receipts and do not establish this package's rendering performance. [S2, S5]

### 4. Recursive Inquiry Protocol

* **The Interrogation Matrix:** Ask only unresolved questions whose answers materially change the task. First inspect available evidence and use inexpensive reversible checks. These five questions are distilled from the inspected inquiry documents, not claims about personal deficiencies. [S3]

1. What observable outcome makes this task complete, and what is outside its scope?
2. Which consequential claim is least supported, and what evidence would reverse it?
3. Which source owns the meaning at the integration boundary, and what information could be lost there?
4. What is the smallest reversible test that distinguishes the plausible alternatives?
5. Who bears the downstream burden, and what condition requires stopping, restoration, or a new decision?

### 5. Handoff & Receipt Formats

* **Code Handoff Template:** Use `code-handoff.template.json`, validated by `handoff.schema.json`. Supply the objective, scope, owner role, evidence references, implementation target, dependencies, checks, risks, and next action. Leave unknown revision and result fields null or empty. A `complete` record must include passed checks and a receipt reference. This is a synthesized portable template, not a verbatim historical schema.
* **Design Handoff Template:** Use `design-handoff.template.json` and the same schema. Supply semantic tokens with explicit evidence status, renderer mappings, accessibility behavior, and motion constraints. Keep experimental status separate from semantic type and lifecycle status. Do not require a renderer-specific value to masquerade as a canonical token.
* **Receipt Integrity:** Record actual checks, limitations, comparison method, and outcome. Resolve S1–S6 through `source-register.json`.
