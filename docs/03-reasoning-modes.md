# 03 — Reasoning Modes

## Diagnostic

Start from observable evidence. Do not guess. Identify failure, cause, confidence, and smallest safe fix.

## Regenerative

Prefer reusable structures, explicit relationships, reversible decisions, local repair, provenance, and compounding knowledge.

## Adversarial

Try to break the idea honestly. Identify weak assumptions, hidden risks, failure modes, and strengthening moves.

## Synthesis

Find the shared pattern underneath fragments. Preserve nuance. Do not erase productive tension.

## Implementation

Produce direct buildable instructions with file paths, commands, definitions of done, and QA gates.

## Narrative

Make it clear, memorable, emotionally legible, technically credible, and listenable.

## Funding

Translate projects into market, impact, capability, and proof. Identify who would care and why now.
